In detail

Managed IT, hourly support or consulting in Richmond Hill

Managed IT is one fixed monthly fee for the whole environment: a 24/7 helpdesk, monitoring and patching, Microsoft 365 administration, security and tested backups. IT support by the hour is for one-off problems when you do not want an agreement. IT consulting is scoped work on a single decision, such as replacing a server, moving to Microsoft 365 or answering a client's security questionnaire.

Search for "IT companies in Richmond Hill" and you get all three kinds of business under one label, plus break-fix repair shops. They are all legitimate. The trouble starts when a business hires one while expecting another. Ask each provider which one it actually is, because the answer tells you who is responsible when something breaks at night.

What managed IT covers day to day

Your staff phone, email or chat the helpdesk at any hour, and every request becomes a ticket recorded against your environment. Nobody on your team has to re-explain the setup every time something breaks. Work is triaged by impact: a server that has stopped, or a mailbox sending invoices nobody wrote, goes ahead of one printer. Routine work like new starters, password resets, licences and printer mapping is done remotely and sits inside the monthly fee.

A lot of the work never produces a ticket. Monitoring watches servers, workstations and network devices, so a backup that did not finish, a disk close to failing or a missing security patch shows up before anyone notices. Ask any Richmond Hill provider what they monitor and what they do with the alerts. That part never shows up on a quote.

Security is in the same agreement: managed firewalls, endpoint protection, email filtering, MFA on business accounts, SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain, and awareness training. At the Managed IT + Security level, managed detection and response watches around the clock. If you have an internal IT person, the same service runs as co-managed IT.

IT consulting in Richmond Hill: how a project runs

Consulting is scoped before it is priced. We start with the decision in front of you, then agree in writing what will be reviewed, what you will receive and what it costs. It is billed by the hour, and if the work turns up something outside the scope, adding it is your call.

Most projects start with an assessment. We review your servers, workstations, network, Microsoft 365 tenant, backups, licensing and security settings, and talk to the people who use them. You get a written report that ranks the risks, says what we would fix first and why, and gives rough budget figures. It is yours to keep whether or not you hire us for the work.

Migrations are the most common project we run: email and files from an old server or hosted platform into Microsoft 365, SharePoint and OneDrive, or cleaning up a tenant that grew without a plan. Each one is staged with a written cutover and rollback. If you want the planning to continue afterwards, our vCIO and IT strategy service keeps a maintained roadmap with scheduled reviews.

Richmond Hill business districts we cover

East Beaver Creek, around Leslie Street and Highway 7, is the commercial centre. The office parks there, built out from the early 1990s along Highway 404, are full of legal, accounting, financial services, consulting, software and distribution firms in multi-tenant buildings. The questions there are mostly about hybrid work: secure remote access, Teams behaving for staff who split their week, meeting rooms, and building-supplied internet you do not control. Some of those firms run a Beaver Creek head office with a second location, which means site-to-site links.

Headford, between Major Mackenzie, Leslie, Highway 404 and 16th Avenue, and the Elgin Mills Business Park further north have more light industrial, trades and service businesses. Wireless there has to cover a shop floor or storage area, scanners and label printers sit on the network, and the ERP cannot be down during a shift.

The Yonge Street spine is different again. Historic downtown between Major Mackenzie and Richmond Heights, and the stretch north through Elgin Mills toward Oak Ridges and Lake Wilcox, is dense with medical and dental clinics, law and accounting offices and realtors. Priorities there are EMR and practice-management uptime, PHIPA-aware handling of patient records, and backups that are encrypted and checked.

Richmond Hill's western edge is Bathurst Street, which is also the Vaughan city limit. A technician leaving our office at 7810 Keele St is driving into the next municipality, not across the region. We also cover Richvale, Langstaff, Bayview Hill, Crosby, Mill Pond, Doncrest and Jefferson, and the neighbouring Vaughan, Markham, Aurora and Newmarket.

How pricing works

Managed IT is a flat monthly fee set by the number of users, devices and sites and the plan level. We do not publish a per-user price, because a figure given before anyone has looked at your environment is a guess. Projects, hardware and third-party licensing are quoted separately, in writing, before you sign. Hourly support and consulting are scoped in writing first. The three plan levels are on our managed IT plans page.

We also do not publish a guaranteed response time. Before you sign, we explain how the helpdesk is staffed, who answers outside business hours and how dispatch from Keele Street works.

Richmond Hill IT support FAQs

Are you a local IT company near Richmond Hill?

Yes. Our office is at 7810 Keele St in Vaughan, directly west of Richmond Hill across Bathurst Street, so on-site visits are a short drive within York Region. Most support is remote and starts straight away, and the same team handles the helpdesk, the security and the on-site work.

How much do managed IT services cost in Richmond Hill?

A fixed monthly fee, scoped to the users and devices we support and the services included, so your IT cost is known before the month starts. Call (289) 582-9930 for a quote based on what you actually run.

Can we hire you for IT consulting without a managed IT contract?

Yes. Assessments, Microsoft 365 or cloud migrations and other one-off projects can be engaged on their own. Consulting is billed by the hour and each piece of work is agreed in writing before it starts.

What does an IT assessment for a Richmond Hill business include?

A review of your servers, workstations, network, Microsoft 365 tenant, backups, licensing and security settings, plus conversations with the people who use them. You get a written report of what is working, what is out of support or misconfigured, and what we would fix first, with rough budget figures.

Can you take over from our current IT provider without downtime?

Yes. We overlap instead of cutting over all at once: we document your environment and take over monitoring, security tools and the helpdesk while your current arrangement is still running. Before you give notice, confirm in writing who holds your Microsoft 365 admin credentials, who owns the domain and DNS, and where the backups live.

What happens when we report an issue outside business hours?

The helpdesk is staffed 24/7, so your users reach a real support team by phone, email or chat at any hour. Monitoring runs in the background too, so many failures are picked up before anyone reports them.

Do you work alongside an existing internal IT person?

Yes. Your internal person keeps the priorities and the relationships, and we add helpdesk depth, after-hours cover, monitoring and security tools. See our co-managed IT services page for how the split is drawn.
