Questions

Vaughan IT outsourcing and consulting questions

What does IT outsourcing mean for a Vaughan business?

An outside team takes over some or all of your IT. Fully outsourced means we run everything; co-managed means we share the work with your internal IT person under a written split; project-based means we deliver one scoped piece of work. We do all three from our office at 7810 Keele St in Concord, Vaughan.

Do you offer IT consulting without a monthly agreement?

Yes. Assessments, office moves, Microsoft 365 migrations and network refreshes can be delivered as stand-alone projects. Each one is scoped and quoted in writing before work starts, and smaller ad hoc work is billed by the hour.

Can you provide on-site tech support in Vaughan?

Yes. Most tickets are fixed remotely, and when a problem needs hands on a PC, printer, switch or firewall, a technician is dispatched from Keele Street to sites in Concord, Woodbridge, Maple, along the Highway 7 and 400 corridor and in the Vaughan Metropolitan Centre.

Can we keep our internal IT person and outsource the rest?

Yes. That is co-managed IT. Your IT person keeps the work that depends on knowing the business, we take after-hours coverage, monitoring, patching, security and project capacity, and a written split names one owner for every system.

How do we switch from our current IT provider?

We list everything that has to be handed over, such as domain and DNS access, Microsoft 365 admin, firewall credentials and backup consoles, and confirm each is in your company's name. Onboarding then usually takes a few weeks: documentation, monitoring and endpoint agents, a restore test, password rotation and an introduction to the helpdesk.
