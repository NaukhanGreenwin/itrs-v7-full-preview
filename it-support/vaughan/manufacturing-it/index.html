Questions

Vaughan manufacturing IT questions

Do you provide IT support for manufacturers in Concord and the rest of Vaughan?

Yes. Our office is at 7810 Keele St in Concord, so plants and warehouses in Concord, along Highway 7 and Highway 400, around Keele and Steeles, on the Pine Valley and Highway 27 corridor and in the Vaughan Enterprise Zone are all a same-city drive.

Can you separate our plant floor network from the office network?

Yes. We inventory connected devices, group them by function, put production, warehouse, building systems and office equipment on separate network segments with firewall rules between them, and document the result. We do not program PLCs or change machine logic; we work with your machine builders and integrators on the network those machines sit on.

Do you support our ERP or MRP system?

We support the infrastructure it runs on: servers, storage, databases, backups, the network path from the floor and the workstations people use. The software vendor supports the application itself. When an issue sits between the two, we deal with the vendor directly on your behalf.

Can you cover a warehouse that runs overnight shifts?

Yes. The helpdesk is 24/7 with a technician answering, and handheld scanners, label printers, warehouse Wi-Fi and the systems they connect to are in the standard scope of the managed agreement.

A customer sent us a security questionnaire. Can you help?

Yes. We help you answer it accurately and gather the evidence behind the answers, such as MFA enforcement, backup test records, endpoint protection reports and vendor access controls, and we fix the gaps the questionnaire exposes. We support the IT controls; we do not certify a business as compliant.
