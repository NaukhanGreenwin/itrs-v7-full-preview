How onboarding works

Onboarding takes a few weeks, not a weekend, and it overlaps with your current arrangement so there is no gap in support.

  1. Call (289) 582-9930 or book a 15-minute IT Health Check. You get a written report on what you run and where the risks are, with no obligation.
  2. Assess: a full review of servers, workstations, network and firewall, Microsoft 365 tenant, backups, security posture and licensing, plus the day-to-day complaints your staff actually have.
  3. Plan and quote: a written onboarding plan and a written quote that state what changes, when, and what sits inside the agreement and what does not.
  4. Onboard: we document users, devices, licences and network, deploy monitoring and security tooling, configure backups and multi-factor authentication, and give your staff one number, one email address and one portal for the helpdesk.
  5. Hand over: if you are leaving another provider, we collect domain registrar access, admin credentials, backup access and the licence list early, and take over monitoring and the helpdesk before the old arrangement ends.
  6. Operate: 24/7 support and proactive management, with regular reviews of the ticket history so a recurring complaint gets its cause removed.
