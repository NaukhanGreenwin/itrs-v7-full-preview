Cybersecurity in Stouffville for Small Offices, Trades and Farms

Cybersecurity for a Stouffville business is mostly about the attacks that already work against small firms: a phished Microsoft 365 password, a fake invoice sent from a lookalike domain, a contractor's laptop that has not been patched in months, and ransomware that encrypts the one PC holding the estimating files or the accounting data. The controls are known and affordable: multi-factor sign-in on every account, endpoint protection that is monitored rather than installed and forgotten, email filtering, SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain, and backups that ransomware cannot reach.

For a business that works from trucks and job sites, the laptops and phones that leave the office matter as much as the office network, so they are managed and patched wherever they are.
