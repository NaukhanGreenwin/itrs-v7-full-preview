IT Support in Markham: Hourly Work or an Ongoing Agreement

There are two ways to work with us, and the right one depends on how much the business leans on its systems rather than on headcount. Hourly IT support is for Markham businesses that need help occasionally: a laptop rebuild, a Microsoft 365 licensing tangle, a new printer or a one-off security review. It is billed by the hour, and each piece of work is scoped in writing before it starts.

An ongoing agreement is a flat monthly fee covering the recurring work that prevents problems: helpdesk, patching, monitoring, backup testing, account administration and security. It becomes the cheaper option once the preventive work is not getting done any other way, which is usually the point where nobody can say when a backup was last restored. How the agreement is structured for Markham firms, including co-managed arrangements alongside an in-house developer or IT person, is on our page for managed IT services in Markham. Many clients start with the free assessment and decide afterwards.

Onboarding and Offboarding for Fast-Hiring Markham Teams

Technology companies in Markham tend to hire in bursts, use contractors, and work with partners overseas. That makes account lifecycle the area where small IT gaps turn into real exposure: a contractor who still has access to a shared drive months after the engagement ended, a new hire waiting two days for a laptop, or a shared admin login that nobody can rotate because nobody knows who uses it.

We handle this as a process rather than as tickets. New starters get a standard device build, Microsoft 365 licences and group memberships set from their role, and MFA enforced before first sign-in. Leavers lose access on their last day, mailboxes and files are handed to a named owner, and company devices are wiped or reassigned. The same records are what a larger customer's security questionnaire asks for when it wants to know how access is granted and removed.

IT Helpdesk Service for Markham Businesses: What Happens When Staff Call

Markham staff contact our IT helpdesk directly by phone or email at any hour, and every request is logged as a ticket so nothing depends on someone remembering it. A technician connects to the affected device remotely, with its monitoring history already open, and most requests are resolved in the same session: a locked account or a multi-factor prompt that will not complete, Outlook or Teams misbehaving, a shared mailbox that has disappeared, a laptop that will not reach the VPN.

Overnight requests matter more than usual in Markham, where many technology firms release software late or work with teams in Asia and Europe. When a fix needs hands on hardware, such as a failed switch, a new workstation or Wi-Fi trouble in a meeting room, the ticket becomes a scheduled visit from our Keele Street office. Every ticket closes with a note of what was wrong and what was done, so recurring faults show up as a pattern. We do not publish a guaranteed response time.

IT Outsourcing in Markham: Fully Managed, Co-Managed or Project-Based

IT outsourcing in Markham usually takes one of three forms. Fully managed suits firms with no IT staff: we act as the IT department, from the helpdesk to security to planning. Co-managed suits firms with an internal IT person or a DevOps team: they keep the work that benefits from being in the building, and we take after-hours cover, patching, security monitoring, backup verification and the corporate Microsoft 365 tenant. Project-based outsourcing covers a defined job, such as a migration, a network refresh or preparing evidence for a customer security questionnaire, scoped in writing and billed by the hour.

Whichever model you choose, outsourcing should not mean losing control. Administrator accounts for Microsoft 365, the domain and the firewall stay in your business's name, the documentation is yours, and if you ever change IT provider the handover is a set of files rather than a negotiation.
