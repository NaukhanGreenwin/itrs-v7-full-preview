Scarborough's Business Map

The Golden Mile along Eglinton Avenue East is where modern Scarborough's economy began. Heritage Toronto recounts how the township, short of money as a wartime munitions plant closed, bought part of the site cheaply and paired it with land to the east to create an industrial district it called the Golden Mile. Today the same stretch between Victoria Park and Birchmount is being remade: United Way Greater Toronto counts 15 development applications proposing more than 35,000 homes there, from builders including RioCan, Choice Properties and Daniels. That is a lot of new residents, and new storefront and service businesses, to follow.

Healthcare and education are the other pillars. Scarborough Health Network operates the General, Centenary and Birchmount hospitals, which support a ring of private clinics, labs and pharmacies around each site. In the east, the University of Toronto Scarborough sits beside Highland Creek next to Centennial College's Morningside Campus, and Centennial's Progress and Ashtonbee campuses add more.

Choosing an IT Company in Scarborough: Questions to Ask

Every IT company in Scarborough describes its service in similar words, so compare on things you can check. Ask where the technicians actually work from and how long it takes them to reach your address; ours work from Keele Street in Vaughan, about 30 to 40 minutes from most of Scarborough by our estimate. Ask what happens when you call at nine on a Saturday night, and whether a person answers or a voicemail does. Ask what is monitored when you have not raised a ticket, and what is done with those alerts.

Then ask for a single page showing what the monthly fee includes and what is billed separately, since hardware, licensing and projects usually are. Finally, ask who owns your domain, your Microsoft 365 tenant and your backups if you leave. The answer should be your business, in writing. A provider that answers any of these vaguely is telling you something worth knowing before you sign.

Cybersecurity Basics for Scarborough Small and Family Businesses

In family-run Scarborough businesses the security gaps tend to come from convenience: one password shared by the whole office, an owner's personal email used for supplier orders, a former employee whose account still works, and a backup drive nobody has checked in a year. None of these is unusual, and none is hard to fix once someone owns the job.

The basics we put in place are multi-factor authentication on every business account, individual logins instead of shared ones, email filtering plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain, endpoint protection and scheduled security updates on every computer, a written checklist for removing access when someone leaves, and backups kept in more than one place and restore-tested. For clinics the same controls, with access logging and encryption, support PHIPA obligations. All of it sits inside our managed agreement; the detail is on our managed cybersecurity page.

Cybersecurity Services in Scarborough: Monitoring, Response and Insurance Questions

Prevention is the start of cybersecurity, not the whole of it. Alongside the basics above, our cybersecurity services for Scarborough businesses include monitored endpoint protection backed by managed detection and response, so suspicious behaviour on a computer is investigated rather than just logged, and alerting on Microsoft 365 sign-ins and mailbox rules, which is where invoice fraud usually shows up first. Staff receive short security awareness training, so a request to change payment details is verified by phone before anyone acts on it.

When something does go wrong, the order of work matters: contain the affected account or device, preserve the logs, restore from a tested backup and work out what was accessed. Under PIPEDA, a breach involving personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm must be reported to the Privacy Commissioner and the affected people notified, and every breach must be recorded. The same controls are what cyber insurance renewals and large customers ask about, and we help you answer those questionnaires accurately. More detail is on our managed cybersecurity page.
