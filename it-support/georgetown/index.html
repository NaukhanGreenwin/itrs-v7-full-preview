Georgetown's Industrial Area, Acton and the Premier Gateway

According to Invest Halton Hills, the Town's four employment areas hold about 8,500 jobs, or 44% of local employment. The Georgetown Industrial Area, on the north side of Highway 7, is the established one: 289 net acres, roughly 4,670 jobs and 186 businesses, nearly built out, with the largest share of manufacturing jobs of any Halton Hills park. Tenants range from advanced manufacturers to small shops where one aging server can carry the whole business. Acton's industrial area adds about 1,530 jobs to the north west.

Growth is concentrated in the south. The Premier Gateway runs along the north side of Highway 401 between James Snow Parkway at the Milton line and Mississauga, with visibility from both the 401 and the 407, and the Town expects it to take about 85% of new employment through 2041. Its Phase 2B lands, north of Steeles Avenue between Eighth Line and Winston Churchill Boulevard, are planned for further employment growth. Warehouses there need wireless across the racking and systems that keep shipping through a night shift. By our estimate, the Premier Gateway is about 30 minutes from 7810 Keele Street and downtown Georgetown about 40, outside rush hour.

Choosing an IT Company in Georgetown: What to Ask

There are few IT companies based in Georgetown itself, so most Halton Hills businesses compare providers from Milton, Brampton, Mississauga or further afield. The useful questions are the same wherever the provider sits. Who answers the phone at night and on weekends, and can that person fix the problem? When were the backups last restored as a test? What does the monthly agreement cover, and what is billed by the hour or quoted as a project?

Ask who will hold the admin passwords, the domain registration and the firewall credentials, and make sure the answer is your business. Ask for IT consulting before any big change, such as a server replacement or a move to the cloud, in the form of a written summary of what you have, what is at risk and what to fix first.
