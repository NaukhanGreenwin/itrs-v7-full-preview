Maple and the Major Mackenzie Healthcare Corridor

The biggest change in Maple's business landscape is the hospital. Around it, Vaughan is leading the Vaughan Healthcare Centre Precinct on an 82-acre parcel at Jane and Major Mackenzie, and its economic development office markets the stretch as the Major Mackenzie Healthcare Corridor for life sciences, healthcare and health technology companies.

The corridor is meant to draw clinics, specialists and health technology firms close to the hospital, next to the professional offices that already serve Maple's neighbourhoods. Canada's Wonderland, just south of the hospital site, adds seasonal hospitality and tourism work to the local mix.

Cybersecurity Basics for Maple Businesses

Email is where most attempts on a small Maple business begin: a phishing link that harvests a Microsoft 365 password, or a convincing invoice with new banking details. The basics stop most of it. Multi-factor authentication on every account, SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your email domain, filtering in front of the mailbox, endpoint protection on each device, and a habit of confirming any payment change by phone.

Our Vaughan business email security study looked at how Vaughan businesses publish email authentication, and our page on cybersecurity services in Vaughan sets out the full managed security scope, including incident response if something does get through.
