What does a managed IT agreement actually cover in Mississauga?

Managed IT is not one service. It is a bundle of recurring work, and the useful question when comparing two Mississauga quotes is which items are inside the base price and which have been moved into add-ons. Ours covers six things. First, the helpdesk: 24/7 access for your staff by phone, email or portal, with a technician answering. Second, proactive monitoring of servers, workstations and network devices, with alerts going to us rather than to your staff. Third, patch management for operating systems and applications, scheduled around your operating hours so a clinic is not rebooting in the middle of a morning of appointments.

Fourth, Microsoft 365 and cloud administration: users, licences, mailboxes, permissions, and the security settings a default tenant leaves open. Fifth, backup: automated local and cloud copies with integrity checks and periodic test restores, because an untested backup is a hypothesis rather than a plan. Sixth, security: managed firewalls, endpoint detection and response, multi-factor authentication, email authentication and monitoring, all maintained continuously rather than installed once.

What does onboarding look like when we take over your Mississauga IT?

Four stages, normally a few weeks from signature to steady state. Assess: we document what you actually have, from servers and workstations to licences, cloud tenants, backups, network equipment and who holds which credentials. This stage regularly surfaces what the previous arrangement never mentioned, including backups that have been silently failing and a former staff member whose access was never removed.

Plan: we agree what has to be fixed immediately versus what can wait, sequence the work around your operating hours, and request anything that needs the outgoing provider's cooperation early rather than discovering the gap late. Onboard: monitoring and endpoint agents are deployed, administrative ownership of the Microsoft 365 tenant and domain registration is moved to your business, documentation is completed and your staff are introduced to the helpdesk. Operate: monitoring, patching, backup verification, security management and regular reviews of what changed and what is coming.

One point we insist on regardless of who you eventually choose: your business should own its own Microsoft 365 tenant and its own domain registration. Some providers hold both, which turns a routine provider change into a hostage negotiation. Check this before you sign anything.

Managed IT services vs an MSP vs managed services in Mississauga: is there a difference?

Not much in practice. "Managed IT services", "managed services" and "managed service provider (MSP)" describe the same arrangement: a company takes ongoing responsibility for your technology under a written scope and a recurring fee, rather than turning up when you call. The difference that matters is in what each Mississauga provider puts inside that scope, and whether the security work is part of it or sold separately.

Some providers use "managed services" to mean monitoring only, with the helpdesk and on-site work billed by the hour. Others include the helpdesk but leave backup and security as add-ons. Read the scope line by line. The managed IT quote checker lists the items worth checking, and our guide to what a managed IT services contract should contain explains the clauses.

What stays your responsibility under a managed IT agreement

Handing IT to a provider does not hand over every decision. Your business still owns its Microsoft 365 tenant, its domain registration and its data, and it should hold its own administrator account. You still decide what to spend, which risks to accept and when equipment is replaced; a good provider brings options and a recommendation, not a fait accompli.

Your staff also have a part to play. They report problems through the helpdesk rather than around it, they use multi-factor authentication, and managers tell the helpdesk about new starters and leavers before the day, not after. Privacy obligations for client and patient information stay with your organisation even when we do the technical work. None of this is onerous, but it is worth agreeing up front so the arrangement is clear on both sides.

Mississauga managed IT questions

What is the difference between managed IT and calling someone when things break?

Break-fix support is paid when something fails. Managed IT is a flat monthly fee for the work that stops things failing: monitoring, patching, backup checks and security maintenance, with the helpdesk included. The failures that cost Mississauga businesses the most, such as a backup that turns out to be empty during a ransomware event, are exactly the ones break-fix support is never paid to prevent.

Is managed IT worth it for a small Mississauga business with ten staff?

Usually yes, if the alternative is an owner acting as the IT department or an hourly technician called after the fact. Ten staff who each lose half a day to a preventable outage is a real cost, and the security obligations do not scale down with headcount. If you already have a well-run internal team the answer may be co-managed rather than fully managed, and we will say so.

Do you have an office in Mississauga?

No. We are at 7810 Keele Street in Vaughan, about 35 minutes from Mississauga by the 407. Remote support and monitoring are unaffected, and scheduled on-site work is dispatched to your premises. We would rather tell you that than open a nominal address to look local.

How long does it take to switch our Mississauga business from our current IT provider?

Typically a few weeks from agreement to steady state, in four stages: assess, plan, onboard and operate. We request the handover items from the outgoing provider early, particularly domain registration, admin credentials and backup access, so nothing is discovered missing on the last day.

Do you manage Microsoft 365 for Mississauga companies, or just support it?

We manage it. That means users, licences and mailboxes, but also the security configuration a default tenant leaves open: conditional access, blocking legacy authentication, restricting external forwarding, and separate backup of mail and files, which Microsoft does not keep indefinitely on your behalf.

Can you support a Mississauga company with more than one site, or staff working from home?

Yes. Multi-site and hybrid work mostly change the network and identity design: how remote access is granted and revoked, how devices are secured off the office network and how the sites see each other. Standing VPN access for people who have left is one of the most common findings when we take over, and one of the easiest to fix.

Will managed IT help us answer customer security questionnaires and cyber insurance applications?

Yes. Those forms ask for specifics such as MFA coverage, endpoint detection, patch cadence, tested backups and offboarding. Because the controls run continuously under the agreement, the answers are true and we can produce the evidence. What we will not do is tell you that you are compliant with a law or standard; that remains a legal question for your advisers.

Yes. The security layer is part of the managed IT agreement rather than a separate product. The detail of each control, from firewalls to ransomware recovery, is on our Mississauga cybersecurity page.

Can we pay by the hour instead of a monthly managed IT fee?

Yes, for one-off work. General IT work for businesses not on an agreement is billed by the hour and scoped in writing first. For ongoing support we recommend the monthly agreement, because the preventive work that avoids outages only happens if someone is paid to do it every month.
