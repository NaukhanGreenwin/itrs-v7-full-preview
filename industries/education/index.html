School IT and Cybersecurity Checklist

Most school IT problems arrive at the worst moment: Wi-Fi that falls over when a full class logs in at once, a Chromebook cart that missed its updates, a phishing email asking the office to change a vendor's banking details, or a staff account still active months after someone left. For a self-review of your own setup, our IT and cybersecurity checklist for schools walks through student and staff accounts and MFA, managing Chromebook and laptop fleets, separating student, staff and guest Wi-Fi, content filtering, protecting student records, tuition and vendor payment fraud, backups and planning the summer refresh. Private and independent schools anywhere in the GTA are supported from our office at 7810 Keele St in Vaughan, with a 24/7 helpdesk and on-site visits when a remote fix is not enough.

Schools & Education IT FAQs

How do you protect student data at schools?

We layer encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, email security with anti-phishing, and tested encrypted backups so student records and family information stay protected and recoverable, supporting your privacy and PIPEDA compliance efforts.

Can you support classroom technology and shared student devices?

Yes. We manage shared laptops, lab computers, and staff devices with endpoint protection and content filtering, keep classroom and administrative systems monitored proactively, and provide helpdesk support so teachers and administrators get fast help during the school day.

Do you work with private schools and smaller educational organizations?

Yes. We support private and independent schools, tutoring centres, childcare organizations, and other educational institutions across Toronto and the GTA, with predictable monthly plans sized to your staff, devices, and campus — plus on-site service from our technicians when an issue is best handled in person.
