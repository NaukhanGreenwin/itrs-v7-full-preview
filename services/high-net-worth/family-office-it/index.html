Family Office IT and Cybersecurity

A family office runs like a small firm with a large target on it. We run its IT the way a careful firm would.

In detail

What a family office actually needs from IT

Our lead engineer spent close to nine years as the dedicated IT lead for a Canadian family office and its principal. The lessons were mostly unglamorous. The serious risks were rarely clever hacks. They were a convincing email asking someone to change banking details, a former assistant who could still open a shared mailbox, or a personal laptop that never got updated because nobody wanted to interrupt the principal.

So the work starts with the basics done properly. Every account has a named owner and is documented. Anything that can move money is behind multi-factor sign-in. Changes to payment instructions are confirmed by phone on a number already on file, and staff and advisers leave with their access removed the same day. The principal gets the same protection without being asked to become their own IT department.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is family office IT different from small business IT?

The headcount is small but the exposure is not. Money movement, confidential family records and the principal's personal accounts sit close together, so payment controls, access reviews and discretion matter more than they would in a typical office of the same size.

Can you support the principal and family as well as the office?

Yes, and we keep the two apart. Family accounts and devices are managed under their own arrangement, so office staff do not see personal email or files and the family does not depend on office systems.

How do you protect a family office against wire fraud?

No single control stops it. We combine mailbox monitoring for forwarding rules and unusual sign-ins, alerts on lookalike domains, multi-factor sign-in on every account that can approve payments, and a written rule that any change to payment details is confirmed by phone on a known number.

What happens when a staff member or adviser leaves?

Their access to email, files, the password vault and any portals we manage is removed the same day, devices are wiped or returned, and shared credentials they knew are changed. We keep a record of what was removed and when.
