In detail

Experience from inside a family office

Our lead engineer spent close to nine years as the dedicated IT lead for a Canadian family office and its principal, covering the office, the residences, travel and the family's personal devices. That is where this service comes from. Most of the work was never dramatic. It was making sure the right people had access and the wrong ones did not, that devices stayed current wherever they were, and that someone answered at any hour.

We run private client work the same way: a short list of people who know your household, everything documented and owned by you, and no talk about who we work for. If you already have an office IT provider, an AV integrator or a security company, we work around them and cover the gaps between.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is the private client service for?

Principals, executives and families in Toronto and the GTA who want their personal technology looked after with the same care as a well-run office. That usually covers the family office, the main residence, a cottage, travel and the people around the family, including household staff.

How do you keep our information confidential?

Access to your accounts is limited to the people working on them, and credentials live in a password vault the household controls, never in email. We do not name private clients, write case studies about them or use them in marketing, and we will sign your confidentiality agreement if you have one.

Can you work alongside our office IT provider, integrator or security company?

Yes. Most households already have several vendors. We take the parts nobody clearly owns, such as personal accounts, the home network and family devices, and write down who is responsible for what so problems reach the right people.

Do you visit cottages and other properties?

We work on site at homes across the GTA and at cottages in Muskoka, and support everything remotely the rest of the time. Properties further away are set up for remote support, with site visits planned in advance.
