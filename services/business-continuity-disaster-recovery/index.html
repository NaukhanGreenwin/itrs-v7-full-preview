Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do backup and disaster recovery services in Toronto include?

With IT Rapid Support they include automated local and cloud backups of servers, selected workstations and Microsoft 365 data, a copy kept out of reach of the office network, daily monitoring of backup jobs, scheduled and recorded test restores, a written recovery plan with agreed RTO and RPO targets, and 24/7 support to carry out that plan when systems go down.

What are IT continuity services?

IT continuity services make sure the technology a business depends on keeps working, or comes back in a planned order, during a disruption such as ransomware, a server failure, an internet outage or an office closure.

Can disaster recovery be tested without disrupting staff?

Usually, yes. Most restore tests are run into an isolated location, so files, mailboxes and systems can be recovered and checked without touching the live environment. We record what was restored and how long it took.

What is the difference between business continuity and disaster recovery?

Business continuity is the broader plan for keeping essential operations moving during a disruption. Disaster recovery focuses on restoring data, systems, applications, and technology services. A resilient business needs both the operating plan and the technical recovery capability.

Is having cloud backup enough for disaster recovery?

No. Backup provides copies of data, while disaster recovery defines what gets restored, in what order, by whom, and within which recovery targets. The backups also need monitoring and restore testing so the business knows they can be used when needed.
