What You Can Outsource: All of IT or Only Part of It

Full outsourcing makes us your IT department. Staff call our helpdesk for everything, we run monitoring, patching, security, Microsoft 365, backups and on-site work, and a named contact owns the three-year plan and the budget. This suits Toronto businesses with no internal IT person, or one who has left and not been replaced.

Partial outsourcing is just as common. Some businesses hand over the helpdesk and after-hours cover and keep projects in-house; others keep day-to-day support and outsource security operations, backup or network management. Where an internal IT person stays, our co-managed IT services set out how the work is split so nothing falls between the two teams. The test for any split is simple: every system has one named owner, in writing.

How Onboarding Works When You Outsource IT

Onboarding starts with discovery before anything is changed: every device, licence, domain, backup, line-of-business application and administrative account is recorded, along with who currently holds the keys to each. That record is written down and the gaps are named, so problems the outgoing arrangement left behind are found at the start rather than during the first incident.

Monitoring, patching, backup checks and security tooling then go in alongside whatever is in place, so there is no window with nothing watching. Staff are told how to reach the helpdesk and what to expect from it. The last stage is handover: administrative credentials, domain registrations, Microsoft 365 tenant ownership and licence agreements are confirmed in your company's name. Our guide on how to switch IT providers covers the same sequence from the buyer's side.

What to Look For in a Toronto IT Outsourcing Company

Ask where the technicians physically work and how they reach your offices, because on-site cover across Toronto depends on it. Ask what is monitored when nobody has raised a ticket and who reads the alerts at 2 am. Ask for one page that shows what the monthly fee includes and what is billed separately; projects, hardware and licensing usually are, and that is fine as long as it is written down before you sign.

Then ask about the exit. Your domain, Microsoft 365 tenant, backups and documentation should belong to your business, and the agreement should say how they are handed over if you leave. A provider who is vague about leaving is telling you how the relationship will feel once you are in it. Our IT outsourcing guide for Toronto businesses goes through the contract terms in more detail.

Outsourced IT or an In-House Hire

One internal IT employee covers business hours, takes holidays and eventually leaves, often with the only copy of the passwords. An outsourced team covers nights, weekends and absences, brings security and cloud specialists one person cannot be, and keeps documentation that does not walk out of the door. What it cannot do is sit in your office every day, which is why many growing businesses end up with both.

The honest comparison is total cost and coverage, not salary against monthly fee: tooling, training, after-hours cover and the cost of a vacancy all sit on the in-house side. Our comparison of managed IT services and in-house IT works through it. If you want a number for your own business, the first assessment is free; outside an agreement, one-off work is billed by the hour.

IT Outsourcing Services FAQs

What are IT outsourcing services?

IT outsourcing means a specialist provider takes responsibility for some or all of your business technology. IT Rapid Support can act as your complete IT department, covering user support, monitoring, maintenance, cybersecurity, cloud, backups, and IT planning.

What IT work can we outsource?

You can outsource the day-to-day helpdesk, device and network monitoring, patching, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365 and cloud administration, backup and recovery, user onboarding and offboarding, on-site support, and ongoing technology planning.

Can outsourced IT work alongside an internal IT employee or team?

Yes. We can provide the complete IT function or work alongside internal staff by adding 24/7 coverage, specialist security and cloud support, monitoring, tools, and extra capacity when the internal team needs it.

Do you provide on-site support across Toronto and the GTA?

Yes. Most requests can be resolved quickly through the 24/7 remote helpdesk, and our team provides scheduled and emergency on-site support across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area when hands-on work is the best solution.

How much do IT outsourcing services cost in Toronto?

Fully outsourced IT is a flat monthly fee set by the number of users, devices and sites, how much of the security stack is included and what after-hours and on-site cover you need. We do not publish a per-user figure because a price quoted before anyone has looked at your environment is a guess. One-off work outside an agreement is billed by the hour, and the first assessment is free.

Do we lose control of our systems if we outsource IT?

You should not, and the agreement should say so. Your domain, Microsoft 365 tenant, backups, licences and documentation stay registered to your business, a senior person at your company keeps administrative access, and changes are recorded so you can see what was done and why. Outsourcing transfers the work, not the ownership.

Can we outsource only the helpdesk or after-hours support?

Yes. Some businesses outsource only the helpdesk, only after-hours cover, or only security monitoring and keep the rest in-house. The scope is written down system by system, so it is clear who owns each piece and nothing is left for both teams to assume the other is handling.
