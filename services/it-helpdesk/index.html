What Our 24/7 IT Help Desk Handles

Most help desk work is ordinary, and it matters because it is constant. A typical week covers password resets and locked accounts, multi-factor authentication prompts on a new phone, Outlook and Teams problems, shared mailbox and calendar permissions, printers and scanners, VPN and remote access, slow or failing laptops, software installs, and new starters who need an account, a device and the right access on day one.

The less ordinary tickets are where a help desk earns its fee: the fault that only affects one user, the application that breaks after an update, the Wi-Fi that drops in one corner of the office. Those are logged with what was tried, so a recurring problem is fixed at the cause instead of being reset every Monday. Anything that looks like a security event, such as a suspicious sign-in or a staff member who entered a password on a strange page, is treated as urgent and handed to the people who run our managed security services.

IT Help Desk vs IT Service Desk: What the Terms Mean

The two terms are often used interchangeably, and for a small or mid-sized business the difference is mostly one of scope. A help desk fixes problems: something is broken and a user needs it working. A service desk also handles requests and changes: new users, departures, access to a folder, a new laptop, a licence change, and keeps a record of what was done and why.

In practice our 24/7 service desk does both through the same contact points and the same team. What you should expect from either is a ticket for every request, a priority set by business impact rather than by who shouted loudest, and a person who owns the ticket until it is closed. If you are comparing providers, ask to see what a closed ticket looks like; the notes tell you more about the service than the brochure does.

Is your help desk really available 24/7?

Yes. Our help desk provides round-the-clock support so your team can reach a technician any time, including evenings, weekends, and holidays, instead of waiting until the next business day.

How fast do you respond to support requests?

We prioritize rapid response and resolve most issues quickly over the phone or remotely. When a problem needs to be handled in person, we dispatch on-site service across Toronto and the GTA.

Can your help desk support remote and hybrid teams?

Yes. We support staff working in the office, from home, and on the road, including secure remote access, Microsoft 365, and the devices and applications your team relies on.

Do you handle everyday requests like new users and password resets?

Yes. Alongside troubleshooting, we handle user onboarding and offboarding, account and password support, software and access requests, and the routine tasks that keep your business moving.

What is the difference between an IT help desk and an IT service desk?

A help desk fixes problems, such as a locked account or a printer that will not print. A service desk also handles requests and changes, such as new users, departures, access and licence changes, and records what was done. Our 24/7 help desk does both through the same phone number, email and ticket portal, with every request logged and owned by a technician until it is closed.

Can you take over the help desk from our current IT provider?
