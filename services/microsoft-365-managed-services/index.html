Microsoft 365 Managed Services FAQs

What is included in Microsoft 365 managed services?

Managed services can include user onboarding and offboarding, mailbox and access administration, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive support, security configuration, email protection, backup and recovery, monitoring, and 24/7 user support.

How is managed Microsoft 365 different from a migration?

A migration is the project that moves email, files, users, or applications into Microsoft 365 or Azure. Managed Microsoft 365 is the ongoing administration, security, maintenance, and user support that keeps the environment reliable after the move.

Can you secure an existing Microsoft 365 tenant?

Yes. We can review an existing environment and manage practical controls such as multi-factor authentication, conditional access, administrative permissions, email security, sharing settings, logging, and backup based on the business and its risks.

Do you provide Microsoft 365 managed services outside Ontario?

Yes. Microsoft 365 is administered in the cloud, so tenant administration, security configuration, backup and helpdesk support are delivered remotely to businesses anywhere in Canada. On-site visits are available across Toronto and the GTA; outside that area the service is remote.

Can our Microsoft 365 data stay in Canada?

Microsoft offers a Canadian data location for Microsoft 365, and the tenant setting shows where your core data is stored. We check it during onboarding, and if your business needs data kept in Canada we take that into account when choosing where the separate Microsoft 365 backup is stored.

How are Microsoft 365 managed services priced?

Microsoft 365 management is part of our managed IT plans, which are a fixed monthly fee driven by users, devices and the level of coverage; Microsoft licensing is normally billed separately. One-off work such as a tenant security review outside an agreement is billed by the hour and scoped in writing first.

Do you support Microsoft 365 users around the clock?

Yes. IT Rapid Support provides a 24/7 helpdesk for Microsoft 365 and broader IT issues, with remote support and on-site service available across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area when hands-on help is needed.
