Overview

Co-managed IT services are an arrangement in which an outside IT provider works alongside your in-house IT staff, taking on agreed responsibilities such as after-hours support, security tooling and patching, while your own team keeps control of the systems and priorities it knows best. IT Rapid Support is a co-managed IT provider for businesses in Toronto, Vaughan and across the GTA, working from our office at 7810 Keele St with 24/7 helpdesk backup, monitoring and patching, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365 and cloud support, project capacity and local on-site service.

What a Co-Managed IT Provider Does vs Your In-House IT Team

In a working week, the difference looks like this. Overnight, our monitoring watches servers, devices and backups and our helpdesk takes the calls; patch cycles are scheduled, tested and reported; security alerts are triaged before anyone in the office logs on. Your in-house IT person starts the day with a list of what happened, instead of an inbox of problems nobody saw.

During the day your team runs the business-specific work: new starters and leavers, the line-of-business applications, vendor relationships, budget and the projects they want to own. When something needs more depth than one or two people have, such as a firewall rebuild, a Microsoft 365 licensing or security problem, or a server that will not come back, they escalate to our senior engineers rather than working it out alone.

Co-Managed IT Security: Who Owns Each Control

Co-managed IT security fails when both teams assume the other one is watching. We avoid that by naming an owner for each control: multi-factor authentication and conditional access, endpoint detection and response, patching, email filtering with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, backups and restore tests, firewall rules and remote access. For most clients we run the tools and the routine checks, and your team approves the changes that affect users.

The agreement also says what happens during an incident: who responds to an alert at 3 a.m., who may isolate a laptop or disable an account without asking first, and who calls the insurer. Businesses that need round-the-clock detection add managed threat detection and response, and the same runbook covers both teams.

24/7 Co-Managed IT Support and After-Hours Coverage

A one- or two-person IT team cannot answer the phone around the clock, take holidays and be off sick without leaving a gap. In a co-managed agreement our helpdesk covers nights, weekends, statutory holidays and the weeks your staff are away, working from the same documentation and ticket history, so a user calling at 7 a.m. on a Sunday gets an answer consistent with what your team would give.

After-hours work follows a runbook your team approves in advance. Routine fixes are done and reported; anything that needs a business decision, such as restoring a production system or taking a server offline, goes to your named on-call contact first. When the fault is physical, a technician can be dispatched from our office in Vaughan.

How to Choose a Co-Managed IT Provider

The questions that matter are practical. Will the provider write down who owns each responsibility before work starts? Can your staff see the tickets, alerts and patch reports for the areas the provider handles? Do administrator accounts for your Microsoft 365 tenant, domain and firewall stay in your company's name? Is the after-hours helpdesk actually staffed, and can someone come on site in the GTA when hardware fails?

Ask too what happens if you end the arrangement: you should get the documentation, passwords and configuration notes back in a usable form. What Your IT Team Keeps and What We Take On

Co-managed IT works when the split follows what each side is best placed to do. Your internal people keep the work that depends on being inside the business: the relationships with staff and managers, the line-of-business applications only your company runs, priorities and budget, and the project work they want to own. They are the ones who know why the warehouse scanner is configured the way it is, and that knowledge should not be outsourced. If you would rather hand over the whole function, our IT outsourcing services page covers full outsourcing.

We take the work that does not fit one or two people. Our Co-Managed IT plan covers help desk overflow and after-hours coverage, patch management and update monitoring, managed endpoint protection (EDR), Microsoft 365 and cloud administration support, backup monitoring and restore testing, and an escalation path to senior engineers. Those are the jobs that are either constant, such as patching, or that need cover at hours and depth an internal person cannot sustain, such as a security alert at 2 am or a Microsoft 365 problem beyond day-to-day administration.

Typical Co-Managed IT Split Models

Help desk overflow and after-hours is the most common starting point. Your IT person handles calls during the working day and we take the overflow when they are busy, plus evenings, weekends, vacations and sick days, working from the same documentation so users get a consistent answer.

Infrastructure and security is the second model. Your team owns users, applications and projects, and we run the background work: patching, endpoint protection, backup monitoring and restore tests, and the security tooling that is uneconomical for a small team to buy and watch. Businesses that need round-the-clock threat detection and response add it through our managed security services, which is how co-managed IT security usually works in practice.

Project capacity is the third. Your team stays on operations while we deliver a defined project, such as a Microsoft 365 migration, a firewall replacement or a new office build-out, then hand back documented systems. Many clients combine models, and the split is expected to change as your team grows or shrinks.

Tooling, Access and Documentation

Co-managed IT fails quietly when the two teams work from different information, so access and documentation are set up before anything else. We deploy our monitoring and management agent to the devices in scope, agree where tickets live so your staff can see what we are working on, and give each technician a named administrator account with multi-factor authentication rather than a shared login.

Ownership stays with you. Administrator accounts for your Microsoft 365 tenant, domain registration and firewall remain in your company's name, our access is limited to what the agreed scope needs, and changes are recorded where your team can read them. Network diagrams, passwords and configuration notes live in a shared documentation system, so if either side is unavailable the other can still work.

Escalation: Who Does What When Something Breaks

Every co-managed agreement includes a written escalation path. It says which problems we fix on our own, which we fix and report, and which need a decision from your named contact before we act, for example restoring a production server, isolating a device for a suspected compromise or taking a system offline during business hours.

Outside working hours our help desk takes the call, follows the runbook you have approved and contacts your on-call person when a decision is theirs to make. During the day, your IT staff can escalate to our senior engineers for problems that need specialist depth, and when the fault is physical we can send a technician on site from our office at 7810 Keele St in Vaughan.

Onboarding a Co-Managed Partnership

Onboarding starts with a discovery session with your IT staff: what they want to keep, where they are stretched, which systems are fragile and what has gone wrong recently. From that we draft the responsibility matrix and escalation path for both teams to agree before anything is switched on.

Then comes the practical work: agents deployed, access set up, documentation reviewed and gaps filled, backups checked with a test restore, and the after-hours runbook tested with a real call. We hold a review with your team once the first few weeks of tickets are in, adjust the split where it is not working, and after that review it on a regular schedule.

Co-Managed IT Services FAQs

What does a co-managed IT provider do?

A co-managed IT provider takes the responsibilities your in-house IT team agrees to hand over and runs them alongside that team. With IT Rapid Support that usually means after-hours and overflow helpdesk, monitoring and patching, endpoint protection, backup monitoring and restore tests, Microsoft 365 support, escalation to senior engineers and on-site help across the GTA, all recorded in a written responsibility matrix.

Do you offer 24/7 co-managed IT support?

Yes. Our helpdesk runs 24/7, so in a co-managed agreement it covers the nights, weekends and holidays your own staff cannot. It works from a runbook your team approves and calls your named on-call person when a decision is theirs to make.

How does co-managed IT security work?

Security tasks are split in writing like everything else. Typically we run the tooling and the routine work, such as endpoint detection, patching, email filtering and backup checks, while your team approves access changes and owns the business decisions. The agreement states who responds to an alert, who can isolate a device and who is told, so an incident at night is not left waiting for someone to work out whose job it is.

What are co-managed IT services?

Co-managed IT is a partnership between your internal IT staff and an outside managed services provider. Your team keeps its role and control while IT Rapid Support supplies agreed services such as 24/7 helpdesk coverage, monitoring, cybersecurity, cloud support, projects, or on-site assistance.

Will co-managed IT replace our internal IT team?

No. The model is designed to strengthen the team you already have. Responsibilities are divided clearly so internal staff can keep the work they know best while we handle coverage gaps, specialist needs, overflow, or projects.

Which responsibilities can we share with IT Rapid Support?

Common areas include after-hours and overflow helpdesk, device and network monitoring, patching, managed cybersecurity, Microsoft 365 and cloud administration, backup and recovery, escalations, projects, and GTA on-site support. The split is built around your team and priorities.

Is co-managed IT only for large businesses?

No. It can fit any organization with an internal IT employee or team that needs more coverage, specialist expertise, tools, or capacity. We scope the partnership around the responsibilities your staff wants to keep and the gaps they want us to cover.

How do you divide responsibilities so nothing falls between the two teams?

We write it down before work starts. A responsibility matrix lists each area, such as user support, patching, endpoint protection, backups, Microsoft 365, firewalls and line-of-business applications, and records who does the work, who approves changes and who is told. It is reviewed whenever your team or systems change, because the usual failure in co-managed IT is both sides assuming the other owns backups.

Will our IT staff see the same tickets and tools you use?

Yes. Your staff get visibility of the tickets, monitoring alerts, patch status and backup reports for the areas we handle, and every administrator account is named to a person rather than shared. Admin accounts for your Microsoft 365 tenant, domain and firewall stay in your company's name, so your team keeps control.

What does co-managed IT cost?

Co-managed IT is a fixed monthly fee for the agreed scope, set by the number of users, devices and sites and by which responsibilities we take on. The Co-Managed IT plan is listed on our managed IT plans page, and project or one-off work outside the agreement is billed by the hour, scoped in writing first.

Do you provide co-managed IT services in Vaughan?

Yes. Our office is at 7810 Keele St in Vaughan, so on-site work for co-managed clients in Vaughan, Concord, Woodbridge and Maple is local, and the same team covers Toronto and the rest of the GTA.
