Practice 02

Know what data you hold, who can see it, and what your AI is allowed to do with it.

Privacy obligations in Canada now reach deep into how systems are configured. We connect the legal requirement to the technical reality: where personal information lives, who can reach it, how long it is kept, how a breach is assessed, and how AI tools are introduced without creating new exposure.

02Method

How an engagement runs.

  1. 01

    Scoping

    We agree the question to be answered, the systems in scope and who needs the output: leadership, counsel, regulator or insurer.

  2. 02

    Discovery

    Interviews, configuration review and data collection, documented as we go.

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Evidence weighed against the legal and technical requirements, gaps rated by real risk.

  4. 04

    Recommendations

    Practical, prioritised actions with owners, effort and sequence.

  5. 05

    Report & follow-through

    A written report, a briefing for decision-makers, and help putting the work in place.
