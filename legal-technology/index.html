02Method

How an engagement runs.

  1. 01

    Scoping

    We agree the question to be answered, the systems in scope and who needs the output: leadership, counsel, regulator or insurer.

  2. 02

    Discovery

    Interviews, configuration review and data collection, documented as we go.

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Evidence weighed against the legal and technical requirements, gaps rated by real risk.

  4. 04

    Recommendations

    Practical, prioritised actions with owners, effort and sequence.

  5. 05

    Report & follow-through

    A written report, a briefing for decision-makers, and help putting the work in place.
