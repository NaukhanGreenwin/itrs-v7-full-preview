Free Security Assessment for GTA Businesses

Free security assessment for businesses: a five-question online check of access, data, network, incident response and compliance, with a score and PDF report.

A free, five-question online security assessment for business owners, office managers and IT leads: answer honestly and get a security maturity score, a rating and matched recommendations, with an optional PDF report.

How do you manage user access across your organization?

Access control determines who can view or use resources in your computing environment.

No formal access management Users have unrestricted access with minimal oversight. Basic password policies Password requirements enforced but limited role separation.

What this assessment covers

Our free security assessment is a short online self-check of how well your organization is protected. It asks five questions, one for each of five areas: access control, data protection, network security, incident response and compliance.

Who it is for

It is built for owners, office managers and IT leads who want a quick, plain-language read on where their business stands before talking to a provider, or who want a starting point for a conversation with their existing IT team.

What the result includes

When you finish, you get a security maturity score and a rating of Vulnerable, Progressing or Resilient, along with recommendations matched to that rating.

A self-assessment is a starting point, not an audit. If the score raises questions, our team can walk through the findings with you: see our managed security services or contact IT Rapid Support.

The five questions in the free security assessment

  • Access control asks how user access is managed, from no formal management through basic password policies and role-based access to identity management with multi-factor authentication.
  • Data protection asks how far encryption goes, from none to encryption at rest and in transit with key management.
  • Network security runs from a basic firewall to intrusion detection and a zero trust design.
  • Incident response asks whether there is a plan and whether it has been tested.
  • Compliance asks whether a compliance program exists and how complete it is.

What you get after the free security assessment

The result is a percentage score, a rating of Vulnerable, Progressing or Resilient, and four recommendations matched to that rating, which you can download as a PDF with your company details on it.

If you would like the result explained, or want help with the first items on the list, our team can go through it with you; contact us at (289) 582-9930 from our office at 7810 Keele St in Vaughan.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the security assessment really free?

Yes. The online security assessment on this page costs nothing to take. It is five multiple-choice questions, nothing is installed or scanned, and the score and recommendations appear as soon as you finish.

How long does the free security assessment take?

A few minutes. There is one question for each of five areas: access control, data protection, network security, incident response and compliance. Each has four answers, so the time goes into choosing the answer that honestly matches how things work today.

Does the free security assessment check our actual systems?

No. It is a self-assessment based only on your answers, so it cannot see a missing patch or a misconfigured firewall. A full IT security assessment inspects the systems themselves; outside a managed agreement that work is billed by the hour and scoped in writing before it starts.
