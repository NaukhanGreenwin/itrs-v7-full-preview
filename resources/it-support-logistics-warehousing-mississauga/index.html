IT Support for Logistics Companies: Mississauga Warehouses and 3PLs

IT support for logistics companies in Mississauga and the GTA: WMS uptime, scanners and warehouse Wi-Fi, EDI monitoring, freight payment fraud and 24/7 shifts.

Talk to us (289) 582-9930

IT support for logistics companies means keeping freight moving: the warehouse management system, the scanners and Wi-Fi on the floor, the label printers at the dock, the EDI and carrier integrations, and the people who answer when one of them fails on a night shift.

Downtime in a warehouse is measured in trucks, not tickets

In an accounting office an outage is an inconvenience. In a distribution centre it is a queue of drivers on detention time, a wave of pick lists that never printed and a shift supervisor calling a number that goes to voicemail. The money is real.

The practical test for any logistics IT provider is simple: who answers at 2 a.m., how fast can they see your systems, and do they already know how your building is wired?

Keeping the WMS up

Whether your warehouse management system is a hosted platform or a server in a closet beside the shipping office, the same three things keep it available.

  • First, monitoring that watches the whole path, not just the server: the internet circuit, the firewall, the core switch, the database and the integration jobs that feed it.
  • Second, a maintenance window that respects your shifts.
  • Third, a real relationship with the WMS vendor, so that when a problem sits between their application and your network, one party owns the call instead of two parties pointing at each other.

If the WMS still runs on premises, ask when the server was last restored from backup as a test, not when it was last backed up.

Scanners and Wi-Fi between the racks

Warehouse Wi-Fi fails for reasons an office network never sees. Steel racking blocks and reflects signal, inventory levels change the radio environment week to week, forklifts move through dead zones, and handheld scanners roam between access points hundreds of times a shift.

Good practice is a proper survey with the racks stocked, access points mounted for coverage down the aisles rather than across them, a separate network for scanners and printers with its own security policy, and mobile device management so that a lost or broken scanner can be wiped and a spare provisioned in minutes.

EDI, carrier portals and the integrations nobody watches

Most of a 3PL's revenue flows through integrations: EDI 940s and 945s with the customer, ASNs to their retailer, rate and label calls to carrier APIs, customs filings to the broker.

Integrations should be monitored like servers. Every job gets an alert when it fails and a second alert when it silently stops running.

Three shifts need three shifts of support

A helpdesk that closes at 6 p.m. is not a helpdesk for a warehouse. Overnight receiving, weekend cross docking and peak season all generate problems when most IT companies are asleep.

Ransomware and the 3PL

Logistics companies are attractive to ransomware crews for the same reason they are valuable to customers: they cannot afford to stop.

For a 3PL the damage is doubled because the stolen data belongs to customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does IT support for logistics companies include?

It covers the systems a warehouse or 3PL cannot run without: the WMS and TMS environment, scanners and mobile devices, warehouse Wi-Fi, dock printers, EDI and carrier integrations, email security and backups, plus a helpdesk that is reachable on every shift.

Do you provide IT support for logistics companies outside Mississauga?

Yes. We support logistics, warehousing and distribution operations across the GTA, including Brampton, Milton, Vaughan and Toronto, with a 24/7 helpdesk and on-site visits dispatched from our office on Keele Street in Vaughan.

Can you work with our existing WMS or TMS vendor?

Yes. We manage the servers, network, devices and backups the application depends on, and we work with the software vendor directly when an issue sits inside their product, so your team does not have to referee between the two.
