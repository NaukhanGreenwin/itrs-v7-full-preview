Cyber Forensics & Assurance

Independent assurance your clients, insurers and auditors can rely on.

When someone outside your business needs to know your controls are real, a self-declared questionnaire is not enough. We assess what is actually configured, test it, and where the evidence supports it, issue a signed letter of attestation that states exactly what was examined and what was found.

Request an assessment 24/7 incident line
01When to call us

Signs it is time to bring us in.

  • A client's vendor-risk team wants written assurance
  • Your cyber insurance renewal asks about MFA, backups and EDR
  • Leadership wants an honest baseline of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace security
  • After an incident, you need independent verification that it is closed
02Scope
  1. 01

    Security assessment

    Identity, email, endpoints, backups, network and policy reviewed against recognised control frameworks.

  2. 02

    Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace audit

    Tenant-wide review of sign-in policy, admin roles, mailbox rules, sharing, retention and alerting.

  3. 03

    Independent verification

    Post-incident confirmation that compromise has been removed and controls restored.

  4. 04

    Letter of attestation

    A signed, scoped letter on ITRS letterhead, issued only when the evidence supports it.

03Deliverables

What you receive.

  • 01Assessment report with rated findings
  • 02Microsoft 365 security audit
  • 03Letter of attestation (scoped, signed)
  • 04Remediation roadmap
