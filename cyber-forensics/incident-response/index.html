Cyber Forensics & Assurance

Contain the incident. Preserve the evidence. Keep the business running.

When an account is taken over, a payment is redirected or files are encrypted, the first hours decide what you can prove later. Our responders take control of the situation, stop the spread, and handle every artefact as if it will be read by an insurer, a regulator or a court.

01When to call us

Signs it is time to bring us in.

  • A mailbox is sending messages nobody wrote
  • A supplier says they were paid, and you never sent the money
  • Files are encrypted or a ransom note has appeared
  • Sign-ins from countries where you have no staff
  • Your insurer or broker has asked for an independent investigation
02Scope
  1. 01

    Triage and containment

    Sessions revoked, credentials reset, malicious rules and forwarding removed, affected hosts isolated, without destroying the evidence you will need.

  2. 02

    Scoping

    We establish which accounts, devices and data were touched, and just as importantly which were not.

  3. 03

    Eradication and recovery

    Root cause closed, systems restored from known-good backups, controls hardened before staff return to normal work.

  4. 04

    Communication

    Plain-language briefings for leadership, coordination with your counsel, insurer and broker, and support for notification decisions.

03Deliverables

What you receive.

  • 01Incident timeline (UTC, source-referenced)
  • 02Investigation report with root cause
  • 03Affected-party analysis for notification decisions
  • 04Remediation plan and closure letter
