Microsoft 365 Forensic Investigation

Evidence-led investigation of Microsoft 365 incidents, from the first sign-in to the final report.

24/7 (289) 582-9930Report an incident

We investigate Microsoft 365 and Entra ID incidents for organisations in Toronto and across the GTA: compromised mailboxes, stolen session tokens, malicious apps and data taken from SharePoint or OneDrive. Each investigation preserves the tenant's logs before they expire and ends in a written timeline that your leadership, counsel and insurer can rely on.

Process

How a Microsoft 365 investigation runs

  1. 01

    Preserve

    Audit, sign-in and mailbox records exported and hashed before retention runs out.

  2. 02

    Contain

    Sessions revoked, attacker rules, methods and app grants removed.

  3. 03

    Reconstruct

    Every source merged into one UTC timeline of access and actions.

  4. 04

    Report

    Findings, evidence gaps and fixes in writing for counsel and insurers.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What should we preserve before calling you?

Nothing that delays the call. If you can, export the unified audit log and Entra sign-in logs for the affected users and take screenshots of suspicious inbox rules before anyone deletes them. We will collect complete, hashed copies as the first step.

How is this different from your digital forensics service?

Digital forensics covers devices, Google Workspace and cloud evidence in general. These pages describe the specific Microsoft 365 incident types we investigate most and what evidence each one relies on.

Can you investigate if we are not an existing client?

Yes. We need a Global Reader or similar delegated access, or an export from your current IT provider, and we work alongside them without changing anything until containment is agreed.

How long does a Microsoft 365 investigation take?

Preservation and containment usually happen on the first day. A full timeline depends on the number of accounts, the date range and how much log history the tenant kept, and we give a scope estimate once we have seen it.
