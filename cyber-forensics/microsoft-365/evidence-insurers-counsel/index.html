Questions

Frequently asked questions

Should the investigation be engaged through our lawyer?

If litigation, regulatory scrutiny or a notification decision is likely, many organisations have counsel retain the investigator so the work is done to support legal advice. Whether privilege applies is a question for your counsel; we work under whichever structure they set.

Our insurer has a panel. Can you still help?

Call your insurer or breach coach first, as some policies require panel firms. We can preserve evidence immediately, work alongside the panel firm, or hand over complete, hashed exports so nothing is lost while that is decided.

Do you decide whether we have to notify a breach?

No. We establish the facts: whose data, which records, and whether access is shown or only possible. Counsel uses those facts to assess real risk of significant harm and reporting duties under PIPEDA or other applicable law.

What does the final report contain?

A summary for decision-makers, the scope and sources reviewed, a UTC timeline, findings tied to specific records, evidence gaps, and the containment and hardening steps taken. Exports and their hashes are listed so another examiner can check the work.
