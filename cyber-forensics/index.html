02Method

How an investigation runs.

  1. 01

    Triage

    A responder takes the call, establishes what is happening and what must not be touched, and agrees immediate priorities with you.

  2. 02

    Containment

    Access revoked, sessions terminated, affected systems isolated, with volatile evidence captured before it disappears.

  3. 03

    Evidence acquisition & chain of custody

    Logs, mailboxes and devices preserved with write protection, hash-verified and logged from collection onward.

  4. 04

    Analysis

    Artefacts correlated into a single UTC timeline: entry point, actions taken, data touched, and what was not affected.

  5. 05

    Report & attestation

    A written report for leadership, counsel and insurer, a remediation plan, and attestation when the evidence supports it.
